Professional Self-Assessment

Throughout my Computer Science program, I have developed knowledge and practical skills in programming, software development, databases, algorithms and data structures, software testing, and data analysis. I began studying Computer Science at Massasoit Community College in 2021 and transferred to Southern New Hampshire University in 2023 to continue working toward my bachelor's degree. My coursework has helped me understand how different areas of computer science work together to solve problems.

My projects have given me opportunities to apply what I learned in practical situations. Through mobile application development, I explored application structure, user interfaces, and object-oriented programming. Through software testing, I gained experience with testing program functionality and evaluating code. My database project allowed me to work with MongoDB, Python, PyMongo, queries, and data visualization. These experiences have helped me develop a broader understanding of computer science.

I have also learned that developing a working application involves more than writing code. Software needs to be organized, tested, understandable, and designed with its intended users in mind. Working on my capstone enhancements has encouraged me to review my previous work, identify opportunities for improvement, and apply feedback as I develop my projects.

My experience with databases and data analysis has strengthened my interest in using technology to organize information and support problem-solving. I am also interested in programming and web development. Although I am still exploring which career path I want to pursue, I hope my Computer Science degree will prepare me for a variety of opportunities, including software development and data-related positions.

This ePortfolio brings together three projects that represent my development in software engineering and design, algorithms and data structures, and databases. Each project demonstrates a different aspect of my learning. Together, they provide examples of how I have applied programming concepts, considered software quality, worked with data, and improved my technical skills throughout the program.

As I continue developing professionally, I want to strengthen my technical abilities, improve my communication and collaboration skills, and continue learning about secure and reliable software development. I view this portfolio as evidence of my progress and as a foundation for my future career.
